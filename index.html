Common questions

01 Is Pill Buddy medical advice?

No. Pill Buddy is an educational reminder app, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat, or replace your prescriber, pharmacist, or the paper insert that came with your pill pack. When in doubt, call your pharmacist — they answer this exact question every day.

02 Does Pill Buddy collect or share my data?

No. Everything — your pill type, pack start date, and history — stays on your device in local storage. There's no account, no analytics, no server, and nothing is ever sent anywhere.

03 My reminder didn't fire. What do I check?

Open Settings → Notifications in Pill Buddy first — if it says "Off," go to iPhone Settings → Pill Buddy → Notifications and allow alerts, sounds, and banners. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device, so Low Power Mode and background refresh restrictions can occasionally delay them.

04 I picked the wrong pill type or pack start date. Can I fix it?

Yes — go to Settings → Your Pill to change your pill type, brand name, or reminder time at any time. To correct your pack start date or fix a day you marked by mistake, use My Pack and tap the day in question.

05 How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Reset Everything to erase your history and start over, or simply delete the app — since nothing leaves your device, that removes everything permanently.

If you've taken more than one pill in a day, call your doctor or Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222 (US, free, 24/7) — this page and the app are not a substitute for that call.

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026.

Pill Buddy has no account system, no advertising, no third-party trackers, and no advertising identifiers. It never asks who you are.

What stays on your device. Your pill type, optional brand name, reminder time, pack start date, and the record of which days you marked as taken are stored in your iPhone's local storage. That history is not uploaded.

What is sent, if you allow it. The app includes a setting called Share anonymous usage data, shown during setup and available any time in Settings. While it is on, Pill Buddy sends usage events to the developer's private database so we can understand what actually helps people take their pill correctly. Each event may include: the event itself (for example a dose logged, a pack started, a tutorial screen viewed), the pill type and brand name you entered, the day number within your pack, the time a dose was logged and how late it was relative to your reminder, your current streak, your iPhone model, your iOS version, and the app version.

How events are identified. Each install generates a random identifier the first time the app opens. It is not your Apple ID, not your phone number, not the device identifier Apple provides to developers, and not derived from anything about you or your hardware. It cannot be used to contact you or to recognize you in another app. Deleting Pill Buddy destroys it permanently.

Who can see it. The data goes to a private database controlled by the developer and is used only to improve the app and to understand adherence patterns in aggregate. It is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party.

Turning it off. Open Settings in the app and switch off Share anonymous usage data. Sending stops immediately and any events still waiting on your device are discarded.

Notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS on your device. No notification content passes through any external service.

Health data. Pill Buddy does not read from or write to Apple Health, and never requests access to it.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data. Use Settings → Reset everything to clear what is on your phone, or delete the app entirely. To have previously sent anonymous events removed from the developer's database, email the address below with the random identifier shown in the app's Settings screen.
